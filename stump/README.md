# STUMP!

*A political climbing simulation.* An early-90s-style pixel demo about going from a total nobody to Mayor of Willow Bend. It's satire: a pothole with its own mailbox, a rendering plant with a signature smell, and a mayor who wants a parking garage named after herself.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a browser (phone or desktop). No build step.

## Chapters (demo)
1. **Citizen.** Walk the town, learn what neighbors care about, declare a cause at the BUGLE, collect 8 signatures in 3 days, file at TOWN HALL.
2. **Council race.** Set a platform, pitch neighbors, knock on doors, plant yard signs, raise money at the Spoon, buy ads, survive the debate, then watch election night on WBND-4.
3. **Council.** Break three 2–2 ties. The Mayor lobbies you, constituents write letters, and the Bugle tracks your promises.
4. **Mayor's race.** Take on eight-term incumbent Bunny Thibodeaux.

Later rungs on the ladder (State Rep, Governor, Senator, the Big Chair) are planned, not built.

## Files
- `js/data.js`: issues, neighborhoods, characters, dialogue, bills
- `js/gfx.js`: palette, 3×5 pixel font, procedural town map, sprites, portraits
- `js/game.js`: chapters, walking, talking, campaigning, debates, elections, council

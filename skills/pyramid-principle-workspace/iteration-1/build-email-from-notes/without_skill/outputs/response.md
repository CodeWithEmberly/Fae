Here's a draft. It puts the recommendation and the ask first (BLUF), then the options, then context.

---

**Subject:** DECISION NEEDED by [Dec 3]: FY27 travel budget 10% cut. Recommend Option C

Sir,

**BLUF:** We've been asked to cut 10% from the FY27 travel budget. I recommend Option C: drop one of our two conferences and make 2 of the 4 quarterly site visits virtual. That saves about 10.5%, which meets the target with a small margin and keeps half of our in-person time with the field units. The budget office needs our answer by 5 Dec. Could I have your decision by [3 Dec] so I can submit on time?

**Options considered:**

- **Option A: Cut both conferences.** Saves about 6%. Low mission impact, since we get little out of them, but it **does not meet the 10% target** on its own.
- **Option B: Make all quarterly site visits virtual.** Saves about 8%. Also **short of 10%**, and we lose all face time with the field units, who have complained about this before.
- **Option C (recommended): Drop one conference and make 2 of 4 site visits virtual.** Saves about 10.5%. **Meets the target**, and we still see the field units in person twice a year.

Option C is the only option that reaches 10% by itself. It also spreads the impact so no single area takes the whole cut.

**Context:** We underspent travel by about 3% last year, so a 10% reduction may be less painful in practice than the number suggests.

If you approve, I'll send Option C to the budget office. I can also bring the line-item breakdown for any of the options if you'd like to see it.

V/r,
[Your name]

---

A few things to check before you send:
- **Your decision date.** I used 3 Dec as a placeholder so you have a buffer before the budget office's 5 Dec deadline. Change it to whatever works.
- **Which conference to keep.** If you already know which one you'd drop under Option C, name it. Your boss will probably ask.
- **The 3% underspend.** I kept it as background only. If you want to use it as an argument (for example, that a smaller cut would be enough), that's a different conversation with the budget office and should be stated directly.

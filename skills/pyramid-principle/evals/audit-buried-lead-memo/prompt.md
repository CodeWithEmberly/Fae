MEMORANDUM FOR 42 SQ/CC
SUBJECT: Maintenance Scheduling

1. Background. Over the past six months the maintenance flight has been using the legacy scheduling spreadsheet. The spreadsheet was created in 2019 by MSgt Doyle who has since PCS'd. It has 14 tabs and many macros.

2. In August we had two instances where aircraft were double-booked for phase inspection and sortie generation. In September there was one more. These caused a combined 11 lost sorties.

3. The wing has licensed the new IMDS scheduling module and three other squadrons are already using it. Training is available through the wing in January. Training takes two days per scheduler and we have four schedulers.

4. TSgt Nguyen has also been working on fixing the spreadsheet macros in her spare time and thinks she can have it fixed by February.

5. Also, the spreadsheet can't be accessed by the flight line expediters on their tablets, which slows things down.

6. Request the commander consider whether we should move to the IMDS module.
